# SERTORIUS — Power and Progression System
## System bible, version 2 (pending author approval)

Design goals: progression is the spine of the series. From chapter 1 on, no chapter
leaves Jonah's number where it found it except the two where holding still is the
drama (17, 30). The reader can always answer: what grade is Jonah, what can he do now
that he could not ten chapters ago, and how far is he from the man he has to beat.
The blade is the unit of combat. The Law of Spoils is the unit of society.

---

## 1. GRADE AND BANDS

Grade is a number. Bands are the names people use. They are the old legion ranks,
because the first loricae were military issue.

| Band | Grade | Who holds it | What it means in a fight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tiro | 1–9 | Blanks. Fused at 9. | Plate stops a knife and a fall. Fang the length of a hand. |
| Miles | 10–19 | Auxiliary officers, low Thinstripes, licensed guards. | Stops small arms. Twice a man's strength. Fang the length of a forearm. |
| Principal | 20–39 | Most Thinstripes. Fused at 39. | Stops a rifle. One Principal kills ten Tiros and does not hurry. |
| Centurion | 40–59 | Thinstripes on military licence. Broadstripe cadets. | Plate takes a cannon hit once. A fang that cuts plate two bands down like cloth. |
| Tribune | 60–89 | Broadstripe heirs the day they inherit. Most senators. | Fights in vacuum. Moves faster than an unaided eye tracks. |
| Legate | 90–119 | Heads of the great houses. About thirty people. | Public record stops here. |
| Imperator | 120+ | The Princeps. Rumours of two others. | Nobody has seen one fight in a hundred years. The Ludi forbid it. |

Rules:
- Fuses. A Blank's lorica is fused at 9 by the Flamens at investiture, a Thinstripe's
  at 39. A military licence lifts a Thinstripe to 59 while it lasts. When it ends the
  excess is burned out: "trimmed". Sabinus has been trimmed twice.
- Broadstripes have no fuse. That is the whole point of being one.
- The number tells you how hard they hit. The gift tells you where. The Form tells you
  when. A 30 with the right Form kills a 45 who is careless, and the Censor makes Jonah
  live inside that sentence for five years.

## 2. THE FIVE LINES

| Line | Plain word | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Virtus | Force | Strength of the plated body. Strike, lift, hold. |
| Celeritas | Speed | Deployment time, movement, reaction. |
| Firmitas | Hold | Plate hardness, damage before shedding, endurance. |
| Acies | Sense | Sight, hearing, vibration, sensing other loricae. |
| Ingenium | Wit | The genius. Gifts held, learning speed, settling speed, fang quality. |

Grade is roughly the sum. Old duelling houses have monstrous Force and Hold and thin
Wit. A cutting is lobotomized at the Wit line: 1 to 3 for life. Tacitus has no ceiling
on Wit. Everything else follows from that.

## 3. THE FANG

Only matter cuts matter. Above Principal, plate ignores bullets and blades of steel.
So bearers fight with fangs: blades grown from the plate of the forearm, dark, glassy,
a hand long on a Tiro and a sword long on a Tribune. The fang is the lorica's matter
sharpened by the genius. Its quality is Wit plus Force. A dumb strong lorica makes a
heavy fang. A clever one makes a fang that changes length mid-cut.

- A fang comes out of the forearm and goes back in. That is the template every cutting
  is locked to. A Vibian fang is a thrusting spike. A Claudian fang is a cleaver. A
  Fulvian fang is a whip's length of edge. The template is inherited with the lorica.
- An ancestral fang is three hundred years old and has been in more men than the house
  will admit.
- Plate has seams: throat, armpit, hip, back of the knee, the wrist where the fang
  emerges. A fang through a seam kills. A fang on the plate skids unless it is two bands
  heavier. That is why duels are fast and why footwork decides them.
- Sharpening: unplated bouts with steel knives. Stripes sharpen against Blanks and
  Collars because a Stripe's real fights will be against seams, and a knife teaches
  seams. It is legal, it is sport, and it kills Collars every Founding Day.
- Firearms exist and are for shooting Collars and ships.

### The Forms
Each great house has a Form, the way it fights with its fang and its gift. The Ludi
crowd knows them like teams. The Censor teaches Jonah four and then the fifth.
- Vibian. Verdict stops your lorica for a heartbeat; the Vibian fang is already in your
  throat seam when it starts again. Thrust, stillness, patience. Priscus's Form.
- Claudian. Wall. Grind forward, absorb, never shed. The Princeps' house and its
  clients. Sabinus fights a Claudian Form without the gift, which is why he is only
  the best fang on one planetoid.
- Fulvian. Tide. Speed that climbs the longer the fight lasts. Circle, cut, cut again.
- Aemilian. Mirror. Fight at range, throw, reflect. Dishonest and effective.
- Sertorian. Quiet. The lost Form. Built for a lorica that can put its fang anywhere:
  the blade that isn't there until it is. No wind-up, no forearm, no tell. Nobody has
  fought it in a hundred years because no cutting can Shift. Tacitus can. The Censor
  learned it from a book that no longer exists, and teaches it with a stick.

## 4. THE LAW OF SPOILS

The strong eat the weak by statute. This section is the society.

- Lex Spolia. Any bearer may challenge any bearer of his band or below to fangs. Death
  is the default. The winner takes the lorica and eats it: a whole lorica gives about a
  fifth of its grade. It is not murder. Every Broadstripe heir defends the inheritance
  at least once. Houses have gone extinct on the floor.
- Declined. Refusing is legal and costs a Brand that every reading shows. A declined
  Thinstripe never holds office. A declined Broadstripe loses precedence. The ambitious
  never refuse, which is a lever Jonah will pull in Book 2.
- Upward only in the Ludi. In the public games a lower bearer may challenge a higher.
  He wagers his life. The higher wagers his stripe. The Ludi are the only ladder in the
  Empire that goes up, and the Empire keeps them public so everyone can see the ladder
  and almost nobody climbs it.
- Calling. A Stripe may call any Blank or Collar to sharpen. No refusal.
- The Lesser Ludi. Collars fight Collars with steel; the winner goes on the auxiliary
  rolls. The only manumission most Collars are ever offered.
- Illegal spolia (outside challenge, war or sentence) is punished by unstripping.
- Senate precedence by duel record. A seat passes by blade. The Princeps holds the Wall
  until someone takes it. Nobody has tried in eighty years.
- In the Pit the same law runs without loricae: rations by challenge, chisels for
  fangs, Auger at the top. Jonah's first Pit fight (ch. 9) is the Law of Spoils with
  the numbers filed off, and he gives the ration back, which nobody has ever done.

## 5. HOW GRADE RISES

1. Strain. Near death, survived. 1 to 3 grades per real fight. Cannot be faked.
2. Spolia. Eating a lorica. A fifth of its grade. Fastest by far, legal only by
   challenge, war or sentence.
3. Drill. One grade a year for the diligent.

Unstripping. The Flamens can cut a lorica out. Usually kills. Leaves a white scar and a
hole where the genius sat. The Censor survived it. Jonah fakes it (ch. 8): Tacitus
sheds a false cutting and burrows.

Settling. One day per grade gained. Fever, misfiring plate, wobbling lines. A man who
jumps ten grades in a night is helpless for a week. This is the brake. Tacitus settles
faster because it thinks, but into something slightly different every time.

## 6. GIFTS

A lorica holds Wit plus one gifts. Blanks two, Thinstripes three or four, ancestral
loricae eight to twelve. Learned by strain (the genius invents a gift to solve what
nearly killed it), by spolia (sometimes passed along), or by lineage.

- Plate gifts: Plate, Harden, Shed, Seal, Spine, Anchor, Mantle.
- Body gifts: Burst, Second Wind, Blood Lock, Pain Gate, Long Breath, Bone Set.
- Edge gifts: Blade (the fang itself, everyone has it), Reach (fang changes length
  mid-cut), Cast, Shot, Store, Ram.
- Sense gifts: Lorica Sight, Tremor, Pulse, Heat, Dark, Scent.
- Command gifts (imperium, granted with office, downward only, they are the law):
  Lock, Hush, Brand, Call, Edict.
- Lineage gifts (one per house, named): Verdict (Vibia), Wall (Claudia), Mirror
  (Aemilia), Tide (Fulvia), Quiet (Sertoria, extinct, sleeping in a columbarium).

## 7. TACITUS: THE UNIQUE TREE

### Root gifts
- Devour. Eats any lorica by contact, ten times normal spolia speed, no kill needed.
  Eats raw matter. Eats a fang mid-thrust: catch it in plate and it is gone. That is the
  signature move of the series and it is first used in chapter 30 on Sabinus. Every
  gift a devoured lorica held has a chance to pass.
- Shift. No template. The fang comes from anywhere: palm, elbow, shoulder, the back of
  the hand holding someone's collar. This is what the Sertorian Form is for. Later,
  Shift wears a borrowed mark. In Book 1 it wears a Blank's mark (ch. 5). In Book 2 a
  broad stripe.
- Voice. Senses loricae as minds. Book 1: pressure, position, size. Book 2: wants.
  Book 4: speech. Book 5: it can tell a fused lorica the fuse is a lie.

### What it does not do
- Obey imperium. Lock, Hush, Brand, Call do nothing. A Broadstripe raises his hand and
  nothing happens.
- Have a fuse. No Flamen can trim it.
- Settle like a cutting. Faster, but different each time. After a big jump Jonah has to
  learn his own plate again.

### Costs
- Hunger. A meter. Tacitus eats matter to grow and to hold shape. Empty, it eats Jonah:
  weight, muscle, bone. High enough, it reaches for the nearest lorica without asking.
  Chapter 32.
- Echoes. Every eaten lorica leaves its cutting's wants and its bearer's habits. Varro
  is the first echo and the one that matters: a duellist's reflexes and a duellist's
  contempt for Collars, both in Jonah's hands. Jonah wins his first three fights on
  Varro and hates it. The Censor's first job is to get Varro out of his hands. By Book 3
  the echoes are a chorus and quieting them costs Wit.
- Reading. Until Quiet, any Flamen scan is a coin toss. Hush turned inward buys hours,
  not days.
- Integrity. A genius bonded to a divided host cannot hold plate. Not punishment.
  Symptom. When Jonah lies to himself the fang comes out wrong. The book of names is how
  he keeps whole. Chapter 30: he kills Decimus cleanly, says his name, the plate holds.
- Names. Tacitus learns the world through names and forgets none. By Book 6 it is the
  book of names.

### Stolen gifts
Wit plus one, and Wit has no cap. Stolen gifts arrive wrong-shaped and must be
re-learned under strain. Each one is a mini arc.
