# SERTORIUS — Power and Progression System
## System bible (pending author approval)

Design goals: progression is the spine of the series, not decoration. Every chapter of
Book 1 from the Pit onward moves at least one number, one skill or one gift. The reader
must always be able to answer three questions: what grade is Jonah, what can he do that
he could not do ten chapters ago, and how far is he from the person he needs to beat.

---

## 1. GRADE AND BANDS

Grade is a single number. It is what a Flamen reading shows, what the law caps, and what
duellists brag about. Bands are the names people actually use in conversation. They are
the old legion ranks, because the first loricae were military issue.

| Band | Grade | Who holds it | What it means in a fight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tiro | 1–9 | Blanks. Legal cap for a citizen is 9. | Plate stops a knife and a fall. Slightly stronger than a fit man. |
| Miles | 10–19 | Auxiliary officers, low Thinstripes, veteran guards on special licence. | Stops small arms. Twice a man's strength. Can jump a wall. |
| Principal | 20–39 | Most Thinstripes. Legal cap for the equestrian order is 39. | Stops a rifle. Runs faster than a ground car in a straight line. One Principal beats ten Tiros. |
| Centurion | 40–59 | Senior Thinstripes on military licence. Broadstripe cadets. | Plate takes a cannon hit once. Shatters stone with a fist. |
| Tribune | 60–89 | Broadstripe heirs the day they inherit. Most senators. | Fights inside a vacuum. Moves faster than an unaided eye tracks. One Tribune levels a city block. |
| Legate | 90–119 | Heads of the great houses. About thirty people alive. | Public record stops here. |
| Imperator | 120+ | The Princeps. Rumours of two others. | Nobody has seen an Imperator fight in a hundred years. The Ludi have a rule that they may not. |

Rules readers need:
- Law caps by caste. A Blank's lorica is physically fused at 9 by the Flamens at
  investiture. A Thinstripe's at 39. Breaking the fuse is a capital crime. The fuse can
  be lifted by an office: a Thinstripe on military licence may rise to 59 while he holds
  the licence. When the licence ends, the excess is burned out of him. That is called
  "being trimmed". Officers fear it more than death.
- Broadstripes have no fuse. That is the whole point of being a Broadstripe.
- Grade is not everything. A grade 30 with the right gift beats a grade 45 with the wrong
  one. This is the rule that lets Jonah win fights he should not win, and the rule the
  Censor drills into him: "The number tells you how hard they hit. The gift tells you
  where."

## 2. THE FIVE LINES

A reading breaks a lorica into five lines. The Flamens use Latin. Everyone else uses the
plain word. Jonah uses the plain word loudly, in front of Flamens.

| Line | Plain word | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Virtus | Force | Strength of the plated body. Striking, lifting, holding. |
| Celeritas | Speed | Deployment time, movement, reaction. |
| Firmitas | Hold | Plate hardness, damage before the plate sheds, endurance. |
| Acies | Edge | Senses. Sight, hearing, vibration, sensing other loricae. |
| Ingenium | Wit | The genius itself. How many gifts it can hold, how fast it learns, how quickly it settles after a jump. |

Grade is roughly the sum of the five lines. Lines grow unevenly. A house that has
duelled for three hundred years has monstrous Force and Hold and thin Wit, because the
genius was never asked to think. The Censor calls the great houses "very strong, very
old dogs".

Wit is the line the Empire has spent four centuries suppressing. A cutting is
lobotomized at the Wit line. A normal lorica has Wit 1 to 3 for life. Tacitus has no
ceiling on Wit. That single fact is Jonah's edge, and everything else follows from it.

## 3. HOW GRADE RISES

Three ways. Everyone knows the first two. Only Flamens and the Censor know the third
properly.

1. Strain. The genius learns when its bearer is close to death and survives. Real
   danger, not sparring. This is why Broadstripe cadets are sent to the Limes and why the
   Ludi are fought with live plate. A hard fight gives 1 to 3 grades. It cannot be faked.
   The genius knows.
2. Spolia. Devouring matter from another lorica. The fastest way is to kill a bearer in
   single combat and take the lorica while it is still warm. A whole lorica gives about
   a fifth of its grade to the devourer. A grade 20 who spoils a grade 30 becomes about
   26. Spoiling is legal only in a sanctioned duel, a declared war, or by a magistrate's
   sentence. Illegal spoiling is punished by unstripping (see section 7).
3. Drill. Time and training. Slow. One grade a year for a diligent Thinstripe. Nobody
   gets to Tribune on drill.

Settling. After any jump the genius needs time to integrate. During settling the bearer
runs a fever, the plate misfires, and the lines wobble. One day per grade gained. A man
who jumps 10 grades in a night is helpless for a week. This is the brake on the whole
system, and the reason Jonah cannot simply eat his way to the top in one book. His Wit
line settles faster than anyone's, but it still settles.

## 4. GIFTS

A gift is a concrete ability the genius has learned. A lorica holds a number of gifts
equal to its Wit line plus one. Most Blanks hold two. Most Thinstripes hold three or
four. A Broadstripe ancestral lorica holds eight to twelve, most of them three hundred
years old.

Gifts are learned in one of three ways: by strain (the genius invents a gift to solve
the thing that nearly killed its bearer), by spolia (a devoured lorica sometimes passes
a gift along), or by lineage (ancestral gifts come with the inheritance).

### 4.1 Plate gifts (form and defence)
- Plate. The basic deployment. Everyone has it. Grade decides how good it is.
- Harden. Doubles Hold on one region for a few seconds. Costs Speed while active.
- Shed. Dumps a damaged plate section and regrows it. Costs matter.
- Seal. Airtight plate. Lets the bearer work in vacuum. Standard on ship crews.
- Spine. Plate grows blades, hooks or spikes on demand.
- Anchor. Plate roots into a surface. Cannot be moved, cannot move.
- Mantle. Plate extends over a second person. Rare. Costs a lot.

### 4.2 Body gifts (enhancement)
- Burst. Triples Speed for two seconds. Then nothing for ten.
- Second Wind. The genius floods the body once per day when it is about to fail.
- Blood Lock. Instant clotting. Standard on soldiers.
- Pain Gate. Shuts off pain. Dangerous. Bearers with Pain Gate die of things they did
  not notice.
- Long Breath. Ten minutes without air.
- Bone Set. Broken bones knit in hours.

### 4.3 Edge gifts (offence)
- Blade. A plate blade from the forearm. The duellist's gift.
- Cast. Throw a plate shard. It returns to the bearer if it stays within a few metres.
- Shot. Launch matter at speed. Rare, costs matter, banned in the Ludi.
- Store. Absorb a blow's force into the plate and release it in the next strike.
- Ram. A single charge with Hold and Force both doubled.

### 4.4 Sense gifts
- Lorica Sight. See other loricae through walls as a shape and a grade.
- Tremor. Read vibration through the plate. Footsteps, engines, lies through a table.
- Pulse. Read a heartbeat at conversational distance. The infiltrator's gift. A
  magistrate with Pulse is a walking lie detector, and knows it.
- Heat. Thermal sight.
- Dark. Night sight.
- Scent. Track a lorica by the matter it sheds.

### 4.5 Command gifts (imperium)
These are not learned. They are granted by the Flamens with an office and revoked when
the office ends. They only work downward on the caste ladder, because they only work on
loricae with a fuse. They are the law.
- Lock. Paralyze a lower-caste lorica within sight. A collar locks instantly. A Blank's
  lorica locks in two seconds. A Thinstripe's takes five, and only from a Broadstripe.
- Hush. Force a lorica dormant. Used on prisoners and at ceremonies.
- Brand. Write a mark into a lorica: a sentence, a debt, an ownership.
- Call. Summon every fused lorica in range to deploy and stand. Used for parades and
  massacres.
- Edict. The Princeps' gift. Lock applied to a whole city at once. Used twice in
  history.

### 4.6 Lineage gifts (ancestral, one per great house, named)
The signature of a Broadstripe house. Everybody in the Interior knows them the way
people know sports teams. Some that matter to the series:
- Verdict (House Vibia, Priscus). Stops any lorica in sight for one heartbeat. One
  heartbeat is enough for a Blade. The judge's gift. Priscus's family made its name in
  the courts and on the duelling floor with the same trick.
- Wall (House Claudia, the Princeps). Plate that does not shed. Ever.
- Mirror (House Aemilia). Reflects a Cast or a Shot back at the thrower.
- Tide (House Fulvia). Speed that rises the longer the fight lasts.
- Quiet (House Sertoria, extinct). The gift nobody remembers, because the house is
  gone. The Censor remembers. It hides a lorica from Lorica Sight and from Flamen
  readings. It is the reason he chose that house for Jonah. When Tacitus devours the
  dormant Sertorian lorica in Book 2, Jonah gets Quiet, and Quiet is what makes the
  infiltration survivable.

## 5. TACITUS: THE UNIQUE TREE

Everyone else's lorica is a cutting. Tacitus is a whole genius. It does not follow the
rules above. It follows these.

### 5.1 The three root gifts (nobody else can have them)
- Devour. Absorbs matter from any lorica by contact, at about ten times the speed of
  normal spolia, without a kill. Also absorbs raw matter (shards, casks, the Vein
  itself). Every bearer he beats is a leap. Every gift a devoured lorica held has a
  chance to pass to him. This is the engine of his progression and the reason the
  Frumentarii will never stop.
- Shift. Plate is not locked to a template. It can become anything: blade, hook, rope,
  seal, mask. Later, it can wear the mark and signature of another lorica. Shift starts
  crude and improves with Wit. In Book 1 he can make a blade and a seal. In Book 2 he
  can wear a stripe.
- Voice. Tacitus senses other loricae as minds, not shapes. In Book 1 it is a pressure
  in the skull that tells him where a suit is and roughly how big. In Book 2 it becomes
  words: he can hear a cutting's simple wants. In Book 4 he can speak back. In Book 5 he
  can tell a fused lorica that its fuse is a lie, and it will believe him. That is the
  lever.

### 5.2 What Tacitus does not do
- It does not obey imperium. No Lock, Hush, Brand or Call works on it. A Broadstripe
  raising his hand at Jonah and nothing happening is a moment worth a whole chapter.
- It does not have a fuse, so no Flamen can trim it.
- It does not settle like a cutting. It settles faster, because it thinks, but it
  settles into something slightly different every time. After a big jump Jonah wakes up
  and has to learn his own plate again.

### 5.3 The costs, as mechanics
- Hunger. A meter. Tacitus consumes matter to grow and to hold its shape. When Hunger is
  high the plate takes from Jonah: weight, then muscle, then bone. He can feel it. When
  Hunger is high enough Tacitus will reach for any lorica nearby without asking. This is
  how the crewman in chapter 32 nearly dies.
- Echoes. Every devoured lorica leaves a trace of its cutting. Its wants. Its bearer's
  habits. After enough spolia Jonah has a chorus of small dead things in the back of his
  head that flinch at fire or want to kneel when a Broadstripe walks in. Tacitus can
  quiet them. It costs Wit while it does.
- Reading. On any Flamen scan he shows as an anomaly: "Grade: indeterminate. Genius:
  unregistered." Until he gets Quiet (Book 2) every reading is a coin toss with his life.
- Integrity. This is the mechanic that carries the theme. Tacitus is a mind bonded to
  Jonah's mind. When Jonah is at war with himself, the plate misfires. Not as punishment.
  As symptom. A genius holding a divided host cannot hold a shape. So in this system a
  man who has to do a bad thing and stay whole has to actually be whole about it: decide,
  own it, not lie to himself. The book of names is not a sentimental object. It is how
  Jonah keeps Integrity. He recites the names to hold the plate. When he kills Decimus
  cleanly and says his name, the plate holds. If he had lied to himself about it, it would
  not have.
- Names. Tacitus learns the world through names. It asks for them. It remembers every one
  Jonah gives it. By the end of the series, Tacitus is the book of names, and the only
  complete record of everyone the Empire erased.

### 5.4 Stolen gifts
Tacitus can hold gifts up to its Wit line plus one, like any lorica, but Wit has no cap
and grows with every bond, so the pool widens across the series. Stolen gifts are always
slightly wrong at first (they were shaped for another body) and have to be re-learned
under strain before they are reliable. That gives every stolen gift its own mini arc.

## 6. JONAH'S PROGRESSION, BOOK 1, CHAPTER BY CHAPTER

Two ladders run side by side. The reader tracks both.

### 6.1 The human ladder (the Censor's Cursus)
The Censor teaches the way the Empire teaches its own: in steps with names. Jonah passes
each step by doing the thing for real, not in the Chamber.

| Step | Skill | Passed in | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| I. Tongue | High Latin, courtroom syntax, switching register | ch. 13–14 | He talks the guards into a quota deal in their own register. |
| II. Count | Reading a hierarchy, who owes whom, where the pressure is | ch. 14 | Same scene. He sees that the deputy prefect needs the numbers more than Tullus does. |
| III. Hinge | Fighting a plated man unplated: seams, the blink, the joint behind the knee | ch. 19 | The riot. He drops a grade 7 guard with a chisel through the hip seam. |
| IV. Hand | Poison, seals, forgery, sleight | ch. 21 | He forges Tullus's harvest manifest, which the escape later depends on. |
| V. Bearing | Walking, eating, insulting, being bored like a Broadstripe | ch. 21 | The Censor makes him hold a "dinner" with a rock for three hours. He passes when he stops finding it funny. |
| VI. Case | Arguing a case to a hostile room and winning | ch. 33 | The Heron's hold. Eight armed smugglers. Nobody dies. |

### 6.2 The lorica ladder
| Chapter | Grade | Band | What changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–17 | 0 | none | A collar, then a shackle. He can be locked by anyone with imperium. |
| 18 | 0 (attuned) | none | Deep-digging. The Vein reaches for him and he lets it touch without letting it in. Heals in half the time. Hears through rock. Not a lorica; no reading shows it. First taste. |
| 23 | 4 (unsettled) | Tiro | The bond. Lines all over the place. Plate comes out on its own. He cannot walk straight for three days. |
| 24 | 6 | Tiro | Names it. Learns to hold the plate in. First gift, Plate, badly. |
| 25 | 7 | Tiro | Under the Flamen scan Tacitus learns dormancy on its own to survive. Second gift: Hush, turned inward. He can go dark. |
| 26 | 9 | Tiro | Feeding on shards. Hunger appears as a thing he can feel. Devour understood for the first time: he can feel the shard's tiny dead want. |
| 29 | 12 | Miles | Harvest day. Strain jump. Shift first used on purpose: plate to blade, blade to hook. Tears the shackle off and Devours it: it is a lorica, a bad one, and it is the first time Voice works. He hears the shackle want to lock him and refuse to. |
| 30 | 12 | Miles | Decimus. No grade. Integrity holds because he does not lie to himself. The reader learns that this is the rule. |
| 31 | 12 | Miles | Stasis in the cask. Tacitus says "Name." |
| 32 | 16 (unsettled) | Miles | Devours half a crewman's Tiro lorica before he stops. First stolen gift: Seal, wrong-shaped, from a ship's crew genius. First echo: the crewman's lorica was afraid of the dark. |
| 35–38 | 17–18 | Miles | Settling. Two fevers. The black-market reading: "Grade: indeterminate. Genius: unregistered. Lineage: none." |
| 40 | 18 | Miles | Gifts: Plate, Hush, Devour, Shift, Voice (sense only), Seal (unreliable). Hunger: high. Wit: unreadable. |

By the numbers, at the end of Book 1 a competent Thinstripe kills him in a fair fight.
He knows it. That is why Book 2 opens with him avoiding every fair fight he can and
entering the Ludi at the lowest step, under a stolen face, to eat his way up.

## 9. HOW READINGS APPEAR ON THE PAGE

Block, indented, no ornament. Used at intake, at inspections, at the black market, and
whenever Jonah gets Tacitus to "read" itself as a joke that stops being a joke.

    READING. Pit intake, Metalla VII.
    Bearer: Vale, Jonah. Peregrine.
    Lorica: collar, standard issue. Genius: none. Lineage: none.
    Grade: 0.
    Remarks: mouth.

    READING. Unlicensed. Anvil, lower docks.
    Bearer: unknown.
    Lorica: present. Genius: UNREGISTERED. Lineage: none.
    Grade: indeterminate. Lines will not hold still.
    Force 4. Speed 5. Hold 3. Edge 6. Wit: no value returned.
    Gifts: Plate. Hush. Seal (malformed). Three unrecognized.
    Remarks: do not repeat this reading.
