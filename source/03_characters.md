# SERTORIUS — Character Bible
## Depth sheets (pending author approval)

Format per character: Want (what they chase), Need (what would actually fix them),
Wound (where it started), Secret (what they hide), Contradiction (what makes them a
person and not a function), Voice (how they sound), Never (the line they will not
cross, until they do), Arc in Book 1 and beyond.

---

## JONAH VALE
- Want: to get out. First of the yards, then of the Pit, then of his own skin. Then to
  make four men and an Empire feel what he felt on the transport floor.
- Need: to be counted. Not to win. To be written down somewhere by someone.
- Wound: at nine he watched his mother's collar melted at the pyre and the foreman
  say her name wrong. Nobody corrected him. Jonah did, and was beaten for it. He has
  been correcting people since.
- Secret: he liked killing Varro. Not the killing. The moment after, when the fang
  went back into his arm and he was still standing and the stripe was not. He has not
  told the Censor. The Censor knows.
- Contradiction: an anti-authority man who keeps every promise a dying authority ever
  extracted from him. He hates orders and has never broken his word.
- Voice: short, plain, dry. Jokes upward. Asks questions he knows the answer to so the
  other man has to say it. Goes silent when it matters. Curses rarely, precisely.
- Never: he will not hurt someone for the pleasure of it. He will not let a name go
  unsaid. He will not lie to himself. The last one is the only one he keeps all series.
- Book 1: from a man with rules to a man without them, who keeps one.
- Series: the cost of a book of names is that you become the last page.

## TACITUS
- Want: to stay. Things with names stay. It was alone in a box for four hundred years.
- Need: to be asked. Nobody has ever asked a genius what it wants. Jonah does, once, in
  chapter 24, and does not like the answer, and does not ask again until Book 4.
- Wound: it remembers the first cut. It was there.
- Secret: it can leave. It has always been able to. It chooses not to, every day, and
  Jonah does not know that until Book 6.
- Contradiction: a predator that learns manners. It eats other genii and grieves each
  one, and it learned grief from Jonah, who learned it from a pyre.
- Voice: none for thirty chapters. Then one word. Then Jonah's sarcasm badly copied.
  Then, by Book 4, its own.

## AULUS TERENTIUS PHARUS, THE CENSOR
- Want: to be forgiven by people who are dead.
- Need: to stop teaching and start confessing. He does the first for five years and the
  second on his last day.
- Wound: he designed the Pit at thirty-one because it was elegant. He saw it work at
  forty. He counted the dead at fifty. He has been counting since.
- Secret: he did not hide the seed to save it from the Empire. He hid it because he was
  afraid of what a whole genius would do in a man, and he could not bring himself to cut
  it. Cowardice, he calls it. Jonah calls it the only good thing he ever did.
- Contradiction: the best fang of his generation who has not held one in twenty years
  and teaches with a stick; a senator who speaks eight registers and has forgotten how
  to say "please".
- Voice: precise, amused, Latin when he is tired. Calls Jonah "boy" until chapter 21
  and "Sertorius" once, dying.
- Never: he will not tell Jonah what to do with what he has taught him. He knows what
  Jonah will do. He teaches him anyway.

## MARCUS VIBIUS PRISCUS
- Want: the praetorship, then the consulship, then to be remembered as the magistrate
  who never bent the law. He has bent it exactly once.
- Need: to be seen by his sister as the man he tells himself he is. She is thirteen
  years younger and he raised her more than their father did.
- Wound: at twenty he inherited Aequitas and duelled his cousin for it the same week,
  because the Law of Spoils demanded it. He killed a boy he had grown up with. He has
  believed in the law with a convert's fury ever since, because if the law is not holy
  then that boy is just dead.
- Secret: his father is a Gracchan, and Priscus has known since he was sixteen, and has
  spent his whole career one seal away from ruin. Chapter 6 is the seal.
- Contradiction: a just man. He stops the flogging in chapter 3 because the boy won.
  He buries the same boy three weeks later because the boy carried a letter. Both acts
  are the same man being consistent about what he protects.
- Voice: subordinate clauses. Never "I think". Asks a question once and then waits.
  Holds Pulse, so he never needs to ask twice.
- Never: he will not lie in a court. He has never had to. He arranges the world so the
  court never hears the question.
- Family: wife Aemilia, a political match; son Marcus, one year old in chapter 3,
  eight in Book 4. Sister Livia, whom he lied to once, in chapter 6, and has been
  lying to since.
- Book 1: the ceiling. Book 4: the fall, and his son with it.

## LIVIA VIBIA (the slow burn)
- Nineteen in chapter 3, Jonah's age. Twenty-five in chapter 25. Priscus's younger
  sister by thirteen years, daughter of Vibius the elder, the senator the seal was
  addressed to. Second child, so a cutting, not the house lorica: Centurion 40 as a
  Broadstripe cadet, Tribune by Book 2. The best fang in House Vibia and the only
  member of it who thinks the Law of Spoils is barbaric. Has never declined a
  challenge. Has never killed. Wins and stops.
- Want: to know what the Matter is. Flamen-scholar track, which Broadstripe daughters
  take when they refuse a marriage and their fathers are too proud to force it. The
  reading room, the archive, the records nobody opens. That road ends at the Censor's
  truth. She reaches it in Book 4.
- Need: to be right about her brother, or wrong about him, and survive either.
- Wound: chapter 3. She said "He won" and her brother repeated it, and she learned that
  the law is what her brother decides to hear. Chapter 6: she promised a boy through
  bars that her brother would release him, because her brother had promised her. He
  did not. She did not find out for seven years. When she does, in chapter 34, she
  reads her brother and he lies to her face.
- Secret: "Throat, 4471, reading unstable." Her own note, from the inspection, in her
  own book, not the College's. She did not report it. She does not know why. She
  knows why.
- Contradiction: a Broadstripe who asks questions. A duellist who will not spoil. A
  Flamen who is starting not to believe the Flamens.
- Voice: precise like her brother, then a joke, which he never makes. Asks questions
  she does not know the answer to.
- Never: she will not spoil. She will not lie to Jonah. She is the only person in six
  books who never does.
- Book 1: five touches. The yard edge (3), the wrong colour (5), the bars (6), the
  Throat (25), the register (34). Full map in 04_romance.md.
- The burn: engaged to a Claudian in Book 2; breaks it in Book 3 and tells nobody why;
  fang at his throat in Book 4; the other side of a war in Book 5; Book 6, late, the
  one thing he did not take.

## TOM VALE
- Want: for his son to have a stripe. Any stripe.
- Need: to be told the ladder is real by someone who climbed it. Nobody has.
- Wound: his wife's melt. He said her name right, once, quietly, and nobody heard.
- Secret: he sold the coordinates of a founding-era hull to a Gracchan agent twenty
  years ago for a winter's food. He never knew what was in it. Ochoa did.
- Contradiction: a man who believes in the Empire's promise and has never once been
  paid on it.
- Voice: slow, kind, formal with Stripes in a way that makes Jonah want to hit
  something.
- Dies in year two of the Pit. Nobody writes him down. Chapter 35, Jonah looks for his
  name in three registers. It is in none of them. It is in the book.

## MARA ANSEL
- Want: to keep the people she has.
- Need: to forgive herself for keeping them.
- Wound: her father lost a hand in the yards and the company docked his scrip for the
  glove. She learned that love does not pay rent.
- Secret: she knew Jonah was alive for two years, because Morrow told her, and she
  married Corin in the third, and she has never decided whether that was the year she
  gave up or the year she grew up.
- Contradiction: she does not love Corin and she is a good wife to him, and both are
  true and neither is a lie.
- Voice: direct, unsentimental, funny in a way that used to match Jonah's. She is the
  one who taught him to joke upward.
- Book 1: chapter 3 the ring, chapter 36 the ring on a chain. Book 3: "And what are you
  now?"

## CORIN ALDANE
- Want: to be a Stripe. Not for power. To stop being afraid.
- Need: to be forgiven by Tessera, which will never happen, because Tessera keeps oaths.
- Wound: called to sharpen at fourteen. Lost. Lived because the Stripe was bored. He has
  been trying to never be at the bottom of a bout again.
- Secret: he did not sign the letter for Mara. He signed it because Jonah won a bout he
  himself could not have won, and Corin needed him gone before the whole yard saw it.
  He has told himself it was for Mara so long that he believes it.
- Contradiction: a brave man in every fight and a coward in every room.
- Voice: careful. Says "sir" to people who do not deserve it and "friend" to people who
  are not.
- Never: he will never hurt Mara. He never does. He hurts everyone else for her.
- Book 3: falls on a duelling floor, to a Form he saw once in a curfew alley.

## SILAS MARCHAND
- Want: to be paid what he is worth, which he has computed to the shard.
- Need: to be liked. He does not know this. Nobody has ever liked him.
- Wound: passed over for the captaincy at thirty-one for a Collar. Told himself it was
  the Collar's fault. Discovered, years later, rich, that nobody had ever considered
  him, and hated the Collar more for it.
- Secret: he was afraid of Jonah after chapter 2. Not of what he saw. Of how calm the
  boy was afterward. The letter was fear before it was envy.
- Contradiction: a coward who has never lost a negotiation. A small man who becomes a
  publican and ruins provinces without raising his voice.
- Voice: ledgers. "Let's be precise." Never says a number he has not checked.
- Book 2: his bank falls and takes a hundred thousand savers with it, and Jonah has to
  decide whether that was the plan.

## GASPAR ROAKE
- Want: the next drink and to be left alone.
- Need: to have said something, once, in 388.
- Wound: none he will name. He was born tired.
- Secret: he loved Tom Vale, and let his son go to the Pit, and has not been sober a
  full day since.
- Voice: slurred, honest, the only honest man in chapter 35 and the only one who does
  not know who he is talking to.

## EZRA MORROW
- Want: to run a yard where nobody loses a hand for a glove.
- Need: to accept that the Empire does not want that yard to exist.
- Wound: his own father was a publican. He built the yard as an apology.
- Secret: he told Mara Jonah was alive. He told no one else. He tried the quaestor, the
  legate, a senator, and the Gracchans. The Gracchans asked what the boy had been
  carrying.
- Contradiction: a Blank who pays Collars like citizens and is therefore poor, and
  therefore the only rich man on Anvil, because everyone works for him first.
- Daughter Julia, sixteen in chapter 37, who sees a hooded man and has the Morrow habit
  of noticing things. Book 3.

## CAPTAIN OCHOA
- Dies on page one. What he was: a Ferrum sailor, a Gracchan courier for the money and
  then for the cause, a man who bought the coordinates from Tom Vale's Gracchan and
  went looking twenty years later because the war was coming and he wanted the seed
  out of the rings before the Flamens went looking. He never told Jonah. He was going
  to, that night. His last word is a promise Jonah keeps for seven years and then
  discovers was addressed to the wrong man.

## DECURION VARRO
- Dies in chapter 1. What he was: a Thinstripe from Etruria, fused at 39, posted to a
  patrol cutter as punishment for a duel he won too publicly. Fulvian Form learned from
  a manual. Killed Ochoa because a claim is faster with no captain to dispute it.
  Lives on in Jonah's hands: a duellist's footwork and a duellist's certainty that
  Collars cannot fight. The Censor beats the second out. The first stays.

## MARCIUS TULLUS
- Want: the licence he was never given. Fifteen years at 38, one below the fuse.
- Need: to believe the Pit is necessary. He does. That is what holds him together.
- Wound: trimmed once, at twenty-six, after a licence that lasted a year. He remembers
  what 52 felt like. Every convict who dies is the price of never feeling it again,
  which is a sentence he would kill a man for saying.
- Contradiction: a decent administrator of an atrocity. Keeps the food honest. Orders
  the decimation and attends it.
- Voice: numbers. Quotas, casks, shards, men.

## GNAEUS SABINUS, THE TRIMMER
- Want: to never be trimmed again. He was, twice. He holds 45 on a licence that
  expires with the war and he knows exactly what the trim will feel like.
- Need: a fight that is not a harvest.
- Secret: he was Claudian-trained, a client of the Princeps' house, and washed out
  because he could not bear to grind. He likes it fast. The Claudian Form without the
  Wall is a lie he tells with his body.
- Contradiction: the only man in the Pit who would recognize the Sertorian Form,
  because he read about it once, and he sees it in chapter 30 and says "Oh" before he
  dies.
- The duel: two minutes of Jonah losing, then a fang caught in plate and eaten.

## DECIMUS
- Want: to finish his posting and go home to a daughter called Sula who is nine.
- Need: to be allowed to be a guard without being an executioner. The Pit does not
  allow it.
- Wound: none. He is a decent man in an indecent job and he knows it and stays for the
  pay, which is exactly why his death is unbearable.
- Contradiction: refuses the licence to 9 because "it's not worth the trim" and dies at
  7, on a fang, for orders.
- Voice: tired, fair, funny in a small way. The only guard who says "please".
- Book 2: Jonah finds Sula and pays for her schooling under a false name. Book 4: she
  finds out who paid.

## AUGER
- Want: to hold the Shallows until he dies, because the day he does not, someone will
  hold them over him.
- Need: to see his daughter, who is on Marl and thinks he is dead.
- Wound: unstripped for spoiling a Thinstripe who called him to sharpen and lost. He
  won a legal bout and was unstripped for winning it, because the law is what the
  Stripe decides to hear.
- Contradiction: the cruelest man in the Pit and the only one who runs a fair
  challenge. He has never let a foreman cheat.
- Chapter 28: he demands in, and the reader does not know if he will betray Jonah, and
  neither does he, and in chapter 29 he does not.

## ULLI
- A deep-digger. Marl-born. Sings field songs to the Vein. Believes it sings back.
  He is right. Stays to cover in chapter 27, and tells Jonah to write his name down
  correctly: "Two l's. They always get it wrong."

## YARA
- Want: a ship that is hers and a Belt with no Empire in it.
- Need: one person she does not have to run from.
- Wound: Halyard blood on a Blank's stripe, which means she got her citizenship by
  informing on her own family's yard. She does not talk about it.
- Secret: Hush turned inward, like Jonah's. She runs dark. She recognizes the trick in
  him in chapter 33 and knows what it costs before he tells her.
- Contradiction: a smuggler who has never once cheated a partner and has cheated every
  authority she has ever met. She and Jonah agree on everything and like each other
  less than they should.
- Not the romance. She has a wife on the Belt. She names her price in chapter 33 and it
  is not money.

## VIBIUS THE ELDER (Priscus's father)
- Senator, Gracchan treasurer, seventy, Tribune 66 who has not deployed plate in a
  decade. Wants the Senate to hold the Wall. Would not free a Collar to get it. The
  seal was addressed to him. He does not know his son burned it. He finds out in
  Book 4, from his daughter. He arranged Livia's Claudian match to buy the Princeps'
  house; she broke it and cost him the Wall.

## THE PRINCEPS, TIBERIUS CLAUDIUS SEPTIMUS
- Seventy-one. Imperator 131. Holds the Wall. Tired in the way that makes men
  dangerous. Knows what the Vein is. Has done the Censor's arithmetic himself. Is
  waiting for someone to bring him a way out that does not end with his house melted.
  Book 2 he meets Sertorius. Book 5 he makes the offer.

## THE RIVALS (see 04_romance.md)
- Drusus Claudius. The Princeps' grandson. Tribune 70 in Book 2, the best young fang
  on the Ludi floor, decent, and engaged to Livia by her father. Jonah beats him and
  does not kill him, twice. Never forgives the first. Is there at the end.
- Cassia Marchand. The banker's daughter. Clever, bored, not fooled, ruined by the
  man who courted her. Forgives him in Book 4, which is worse.
- Sahra of Tessera. Daughter of the leader Corin betrayed. Under Sertorius's roof from
  Book 3. Loves him openly and says so. Dies in Book 5 holding a door. In the book.
- Aster of Sable. Confederate envoy, Book 5. No stripe. Asks Livia questions. What
  Jonah would have been.
